# 0010. Chinese on hold, and no Chinese retro web

- Date: 2026-09-28
- Status: accepted

## Context

The site has a Chinese copy, kept for later. Separately, a proposal for
the 1999 to 2011 Chinese web was drafted: portals from the Internet
Archive, a hand-made 2008 QQ Zone over Soapbox, Photos and Stickies, and
a QQ2006 skin for Chat, with Tencent's own art.

## Decision

- The Chinese copy of the site is on hold, and there's no link back to a
  classic version of the site.
- The Chinese retro web was set aside: it doesn't fit the Mac OS X look
  of the rest. The Browser got the basics ryOS's Internet Explorer has
  instead, with time travel through the Internet Archive.

## Consequences

- The `zh` copy stays in the repository, unused for now.
- AI-made pages in the Browser wait on the AI assistant, which is on hold
  until its spending can be capped.
