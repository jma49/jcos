# Decisions

Architecture decision records: why JM/OS is the way it is, one decision
to a file, with what was weighed and what was turned down. They record
choices that hold beyond today; how each part works is in
[docs/agents/](../agents/), and the plan is [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md).

A new decision gets the next number, `NNNN-short-title.md`, with a date,
a status (accepted, or superseded by a later one), and three sections:
Context, Decision and Consequences. A decision that changes is amended
with its new date, or superseded by a new record that says which one it
replaces; don't delete the old one.
